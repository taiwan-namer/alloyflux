Leaders discuss planned summit amid tariff talks

Diplomatic officials said preparations for a planned summit continued on Friday, while trade negotiators reviewed a new tariff statement released earlier in the week.

Analysts noted that markets reacted cautiously to the announcement, with investors watching for confirmation from all parties before adjusting forecasts.

A spokesperson declined to set a final date, adding that logistics and security reviews were still underway across several capitals.

Separately, local election results elsewhere on the same day drew attention but were described as unrelated to the diplomatic track.
